Last updated: 21 October 2026

Who we are

This policy explains how Motooor handles the personal data you share when you join our waitlist.

The data controller is Inari Studio SL, with registered address at calle Lopez de Hoyos, 123, 28002, Madrid, Spain and CIF B87904694. For any privacy question, contact us at privacy@motooor.com.

What data we collect

We only collect what we need to manage the waitlist:

  • Email address, which you enter in the waitlist form.
  • Signup details, such as the date and time you joined and the page you joined from.
  • Technical data, such as your IP address and browser type, which our hosting and email providers log automatically for security and delivery purposes.

We do not ask for sensitive data, and the waitlist is not intended for people under 16.

Why we use it and on what legal basis

We use your email to:

  • Confirm that you have joined the waitlist.
  • Tell you when you can access Motooor and send occasional updates about the launch.
  • Ask, optionally, for your feedback as an early user.

The legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), given when you submit the form. You can withdraw it at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email or by writing to us. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing carried out before.

How long we keep it

We keep your email while the waitlist is active and you remain subscribed. If you unsubscribe or ask us to delete your data, we remove it within 30 days. If Motooor does not launch, we delete the whole list within 12 months of closing the waitlist.

Your rights

You can, at any time and free of charge:

  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Correct it if it is inaccurate.
  • Ask us to delete it.
  • Restrict or object to its processing.
  • Receive it in a portable format.
  • Withdraw your consent.

To exercise these rights, email privacy@motooor.com. We will reply within one month.

If you believe we have not handled your data properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) or the authority in your country.

Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, and our providers do the same under their agreements with us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Motooor evolves. We will post any change on this page with a new date, and notify you by email if the change is significant.

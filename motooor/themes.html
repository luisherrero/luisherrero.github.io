Theme lab

App or Viewer: the panel lists the tokens and text styles that part uses (shared ones show in both) and the previews show it; the viewer's pages in a phone-sized frame per theme. Right-click any element of a preview to see its colours (per state: hover, selected, ::before…) and its text style, and edit them there for that theme. Palette: the raw colours (picker or HSL + alpha), shared by every theme and by app and viewer. Themes: each token points to a palette colour, optionally at a % (opacity); the usage link under it says where it is used; its swatch picks a colour without touching the palette (a palette colour as is, or the closest one, or a new step such as grey-750). Text styles: size, weight, tracking, line-height (a multiple of 4) and case. ↺ resets a changed row. Changes show live and are kept in this browser until the stylesheet has them: export them to _themes.scss, or import that file here.
